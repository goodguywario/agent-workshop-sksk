Speech writer: Nora's story

Six frames from requests/speech-writer/spec.md · Julebord 2026

Frame 1 · Meet Nora

Nora is organising Julebord 2026.

She's the office manager at Mammut. The party is on 11 December at Kåken, with a Viking theme. The party planner has already written the invitation, menu, seating and playlist.

Context: who the feature is for
Frame 2 · The problem

Three speeches, three tones, one Nora.

The CEO, the toastmaster and HR all have to speak, and all three ask Nora to "just draft something". Each wants a different tone and length. Her page has 0 words.

Why: several speakers, each with their own style
Frame 3 · The "oh no" moment

The quick draft would embarrass everyone.

At 23:40 the night before, Nora pastes the guest list into a chatbot. The draft jokes about Ben and Gary's private request and Hannah's EpiPen, and it thanks Victor, who declined.

Frame 5 · The "aha" moment

Three speeches, each in its own voice, and nothing private.

Nora runs /plan-party, and the page fills in by itself. Maggie's speech is formal and thanks Engineering, and Liam's has the dad-jokes. Nobody's private notes appear, because the writer only gets public details about guests who are coming. Nora clicks Copy and sends each speech to its speaker.

Frame 6 · Afterwards

The speeches land, and Nora gets her dessert.

At Kåken, Liam's pun gets a groan and a laugh, Maggie thanks Engineering, and Rachel welcomes the new people. Ben and Gary laugh at the same joke, because nothing in the speeches was about them. Next year Nora will just change the settings and run the planner again.

Success: no private details, no absent guests, right length
