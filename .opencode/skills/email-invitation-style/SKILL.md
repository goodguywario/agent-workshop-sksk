# Email invitation style

The organiser copies the invitation into one email to all guests, so it is written to everyone ("Dear colleagues,").

## Format

- **Plain text** that pastes cleanly into any email client: paragraphs and line breaks, with a short list of dashes for the practical details. No markdown syntax such as `**`, `#` or `[links]()`.
- **Subject** of at most 60 characters that names the event.
- **Body** of 120–220 words, warm and festive.

## Required details

Every invitation states:

- the date and time in human form, e.g. "Friday 11 December 2026, 18:00 until 01:00"
- the venue name and full address
- the dress code, worded as in the event settings
- the RSVP deadline in human form, e.g. "by Friday 27 November"
- a request to confirm whether they are bringing their partner

## Sign-off

The invitation is signed by the boss: their full name and job title, as they appear in the guest list.
